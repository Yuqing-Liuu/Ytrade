**YTrade** is a personal stock research workspace built for individual investors who want to think like disciplined analysts.

Instead of reacting to price movements, YTrade walks you through a structured 9-section research template for every company you study — covering the business model, financials, valuation, risks, and trading plan. The goal is to make sure you've done the work before you invest, not after.

Each stock gets a status (Watching, Researching, Ready to Buy, Hold, or Avoid) and a conviction score from 1 to 10. Everything is saved locally in your browser — no account, no backend, no distractions.
